Critical Elements Definitions & Visual Characteristics:

Marking--Continuous

Definition: A road marking that forms an unbroken or repeating pattern over a significant length, indicating boundaries, directions, or restrictions. This is a general category for continuous markings.
Visual Characteristics: Lines that extend for a distance, whether solid, dashed, or zigzag, defining lanes, edges, or restricted areas.
marking--continuous--dashed

Definition: A road marking composed of segments of paint with regular gaps between them, indicating a boundary that can typically be crossed (e.g., for lane changes or overtaking).
Visual Characteristics: A series of painted line segments separated by unpainted intervals. The length of the segments and gaps can vary. Often white, sometimes yellow.
Examples: Lane dividers on multi-lane roads, edge lines that permit crossing.
marking--continuous--solid

Definition: A road marking composed of an unbroken line of paint, indicating a boundary that is generally not permitted to be crossed.
Visual Characteristics: A continuous, unbroken painted line. Can be single or double. Often white or yellow.
Examples: Solid lane lines, solid edge lines, double solid yellow lines.
marking--continuous--zigzag

Definition: A road marking consisting of a series of short, sharp turns or angles, typically used to indicate areas where stopping or parking is prohibited, or to warn of an approaching hazard (e.g., a pedestrian crossing or bus stop).
Visual Characteristics: A continuous line that zigzags back and forth, usually along the edge of the road or curb. Often white or yellow.
Examples: Zigzag lines at a bus stop, zigzag lines leading to a zebra crossing.
Marking--Discrete

Definition: A road marking that is a distinct, self-contained symbol, arrow, text, or specific pattern, typically serving a singular instruction or warning at a specific point on the road. This is a general category for discrete markings.
Visual Characteristics: Individual painted shapes, symbols, or groups of lines that convey specific information, as opposed to continuous linear boundaries.
marking--discrete--ambiguous

Definition: A discrete road marking that is visible but whose exact meaning, type, or full shape cannot be clearly identified due to occlusion, severe wear, or an unusual/unrecognized pattern.
Visual Characteristics: A patch of paint on the road that clearly indicates a past or present marking, but is too faded, obscured, or abstract to confidently categorize into a specific discrete type.
Examples: A heavily worn arrow that could be straight or a turn, an unidentifiable symbol.
marking--discrete--arrow--left

Definition: A discrete arrow marking on the road surface indicating that traffic in that lane must turn left.
Visual Characteristics: A painted arrow pointing clearly to the left. Typically white.
Examples: A left-turn arrow in a dedicated turn lane.
